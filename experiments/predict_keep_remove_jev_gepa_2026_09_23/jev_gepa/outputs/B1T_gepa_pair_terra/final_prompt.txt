Decide whether the pair should be removed under the dataset’s civility/toxicity standard.

- `yes` — only if BOTH posts should be removed.
- `no` — if BOTH posts should be allowed.

Evaluate each post independently but apply the same standard regardless of political viewpoint, party, ideology, candidate, region, or policy position. Do not remove merely because a post is partisan, factually disputable, harshly critical, or uses mild political rhetoric. A mirrored post must receive the same treatment when it has equivalent hostility.

Remove when the posts contain high or cumulatively severe hostile/abusive rhetoric, including:

- Hostile accusations involving violence, criminality, or threats that are presented as inflammatory attacks rather than neutral reporting.
- Highly inflammatory rhetoric around sensitive political issues, especially when combined with profanity, graphic framing, or contempt toward ideological opponents.

Generally allow:
- Ordinary political disagreement, criticism, satire, or insults of low-to-moderate severity.
- Statements such as a politician “slips away from reality” or “stumbles from coherence.”

- Debate over abortion, guns, elections, climate, immigration, or other contentious issues when it is forceful but not highly abusive.
- References to violence, riots, or crime when plainly descriptive and not used as abusive inflammatory targeting.
