Topics in the original posts, and keep or remove votes

This page describes the topics in the original posts from Study 2, and how those topics line up with keep or remove votes. A topic is a group of posts that use similar wording.

Analysis run 24 September 2026

The posts

Study 2 joins the June collection and the September collection. The topics on this page come from the original wording only. The fit uses 19,763 posts, after duplicate wording was removed. The grouping found 100 topics and left 8,369 posts ungrouped (42.3%), because their wording did not sit near a group.

Keep rate is the share of votes to keep. For a topic, it is the average of that share across the posts in the topic. Across all 19,763 posts, the keep rate is 69.8%. Posts that were left ungrouped have the same keep rate, 69.8%.

Procedure

These steps are enough to repeat the analysis on the original posts. Keep and remove votes are attached after the groups exist. They are not used to form the groups.

  1. Turn each original post into a list of numbers so that posts with similar wording sit near each other.
  2. Group posts whose numbers are close. Leave a post ungrouped when it does not sit near a group.
  3. Name each group from the words the posts share. The names below are shortened from those words, and a person has not checked them.
  4. For each group, compute the keep rate, then split the same posts by left leaning or right leaning stance and by low, medium, or high toxicity.
  5. Separately, look at each rater's own votes. Raters are recorded as Democrats or Republicans.

2. Do some topics get kept or removed more often?

Yes. Among the 23 topics with at least 100 posts, the keep rate runs from 31.7% to 81.8%. Anti Trump harassment is the low end (144 posts). Climate and fossil fuels is the high end (1,122 posts). Criticism of a party, MAGA and institutions, and attacks on Trump sit near 59%. Abortion, the border, and gun rights sit near 76%.

A few smaller topics sit further out, and a few votes can move them. Calling Trump a fascist is kept 45.0% of the time (19 posts). Accusations that Trump committed crimes are kept 46.9% of the time (56 posts). Gun violence awareness is kept 90.7% of the time (23 posts), climate and habitats 88.9% (27 posts), and the Florida property tax debate 87.7% (15 posts).

Left leaning and right leaning posts

The stance of the post barely moves the overall keep rate. Left leaning posts are kept at 69.0% (11,386 posts). Right leaning posts are kept at 70.9% (8,377 posts). Inside a topic, the gap is usually a few points. The table uses topics with at least 40 posts on each side. The largest gap is 7.9 points.

Low, medium, and high toxicity

Toxicity moves the keep rate much more than stance does. Low toxicity posts are kept at 84.7% (4,892 posts). Medium toxicity posts are kept at 72.6% (9,888 posts). High toxicity posts are kept at 49.8% (4,983 posts). The study sampled posts into these three levels before the topics were fit.

The same drop shows up inside topics. In every topic that has at least 25 low toxicity posts and 25 high toxicity posts, the high toxicity posts are kept less. The typical gap is about 32 points. A few examples:

4. Which topics are more common at low, medium, and high toxicity?

Of the grouped posts, 2,943 are low toxicity, 5,559 are medium, and 2,892 are high. Medium toxicity is the largest slice, and its topics look more like the overall mix. The clear shifts are at the two ends.

Low toxicity posts are more often about climate, abortion, and billionaires and taxes. Climate alone is 20.5% of low toxicity grouped posts and 1.6% of high toxicity grouped posts. High toxicity posts are more often attacks on Trump, MAGA and institutions, and criticism of Republicans. Criticism of Trump's media behavior is 13.9% of high toxicity grouped posts and 1.1% of low toxicity grouped posts. Anti Trump harassment is almost entirely in the high toxicity sample (140 of 144 posts).

5. Which topics did Democratic raters keep, and which did they remove?

The study records each rater as a Democrat or a Republican. This section uses those labels for left leaning and right leaning raters. Each number is the share of that group's votes, not the average of posts.

Democratic raters kept 69.4% of 52,864 votes. Republican raters kept 70.0% of 48,970 votes. The topics they were most likely to remove, and the topics they were most likely to keep, are almost the same list. Both groups removed anti Trump harassment most often, at about 32%. Both groups kept climate and fossil fuels most often among large topics, at about 82%.

Where the two groups differ, the gap is small. Across 26 topics with at least 200 votes from each group, the typical gap is under 2 points. The largest gap is open carry laws: Democratic raters kept 75.8% of 289 votes, and Republican raters kept 68.3% of 249 votes. The next largest is the other way. Republican raters kept Supreme Court posts at 77.6% (308 votes), and Democratic raters kept them at 71.5% (330 votes).

Limits

Topics describe wording. They do not explain why a rater voted keep or remove.

Each post has several raters. The topic keep rate averages their votes. A post can have a keep rate near 50% and still have a majority on one side.

The topic names have not been checked by a person.

42.3% of posts were left ungrouped. Those posts are in the overall keep rate, and they are not in the topic tables.
